About

Meet Dr. Sushma Dhakal

Dr. Sushma Dhakal is a Nepali obstetrician-gynaecologist with a Fellowship in Fetal Medicine from BLK-Max Super Speciality Hospital, New Delhi (2025–2026). She holds FMF (UK) certification in NT and anomaly scanning and performs first-trimester, anomaly, genetic sonogram, neurosonogram, growth and Doppler scans, as well as amniocentesis, chorionic villus sampling and fetal procedures. Before her fellowship she worked as Registrar at Kirtipur Hospital and as Lecturer at Nepal Medical College, and as Medical Officer at Patan Hospital and KIST Medical College. She counsels families on fetal structural and genetic conditions and high-risk pregnancy in Nepali, English and Hindi.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When should I get an NT scan?

Between 11 weeks and 13 weeks 6 days of pregnancy.

When is the anomaly scan done?

Usually between 18 and 22 weeks of pregnancy.

Is Dr. Sushma Dhakal FMF certified?

Yes. She holds Fetal Medicine Foundation (UK) certification for NT and anomaly scans.
